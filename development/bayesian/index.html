Provisional assumptions

Discrimination is fixed at a = 1.00, with a provisional 5% lapse and 2% lower response floor. Simultaneous difficulty b is derived from a provisional success-probability schedule at θ = 0 (average-adult reference), with interpolation/extrapolation on the logit scale. Sequential difficulty remains provisional pending separate calibration.

The posterior starts at θ = −2 as a provisional threshold-search starting prior, then updates upwards after exact responses and downwards after non-exact responses. This is not a population prior. The posterior uses exact versus non-exact responses with provisional lapse/guess floors. The threshold controller separately uses repeated exact responses. Location accuracy, omissions, false selections and serial-position scores remain available separately for later partial-credit modelling.

Simultaneous and sequential conditions have separate posterior distributions. The Bayesian posterior is descriptive; the threshold controller determines the next trial.
